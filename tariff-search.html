Method & limits

What this dashboard measures

  • Rows are parsed from the Government of Canada consolidated schedule updated August 26, 2026, plus the still-active alcohol lines from the original March 2025 countermeasures package (beer, wine, cider and spirits, HS chapter 22), which were not carried into the September 8, 2026 dollar-for-dollar list but remain in force at 25%.
  • Sector labels are analytical groupings based primarily on HS chapter, with product-term rules for appliances and farm equipment.
  • The government reports C$27.6B in covered U.S. imports for the September 8 measure as a whole. It does not publish a value beside each tariff item in this schedule.
  • “Trade impact” therefore remains “aggregate only” at item level. Sector bars compare tariff-line breadth, not dollars.
  • The 739-line total combines the 648 lines on the current September 8 table with 91 still-active alcohol lines carried over from March 2025. Each alcohol row is labelled with its original source and effective date. Historical or expired countermeasures outside these two in-force packages are excluded.
  • The Top 10 is an editorial action list. It prioritizes consumer-facing products using rate, visibility, substitutability and covered-line breadth. It is not presented as a dollar ranking.
