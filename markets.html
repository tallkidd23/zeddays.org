How inclusion works

  1. Requests are free. ZED DAYS does not sell listings or preferred placement.
  2. The market should be a recurring public market in Southwestern Ontario with local farm or food producers.
  3. A current official market, organizer, municipal or association page must confirm the schedule.
  4. ZED DAYS reviews every request independently and may ask for clarification before publishing.
  5. Submission does not guarantee inclusion. Listings may be updated or removed when schedules become outdated.
  6. This form is for markets as organizations. Individual vendor applications are not accepted yet.

Privacy: This form does not store or transmit information through the website. It prepares an email in your own mail application.
